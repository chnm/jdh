<p>The <em>Journal of Digital Humanities</em> (ISSN 2165-6673) is a comprehensive, peer-reviewed, open access journal that features scholarship, tools, and conversations produced, identified, and tracked by members of the digital humanities community through <a href="http://digitalhumanitiesnow.org/" target="_blank" title="Digital Humanities Now"><em>Digital Humanities Now</em></a>.</p>
<p>Initiated by the <a href="http://pressforward.org" target="_blank" title="PressForward Project Homepage">PressForward</a> project, the <em>Journal of Digital Humanities</em> is an experiment in sourcing and distributing scholarly communication on the open web. The <em>Journal of Digital Humanities</em> selects content from the <a href="http://digitalhumanitiesnow.org/category/featured/" target="_blank" title="Editors' Choice from Digital Humanities Now">Editors’ Choice</a> pieces from <em><a href="http://digitalhumanitiesnow.org/" target="_blank" title="Digital Humanities Now">Digital Humanities Now</a></em>, which highlights the best scholarship — in whatever form — that drives the field of digital humanities field forward. The <em>Journal of Digital Humanities</em> provides three additional layers of evaluation, review, and editing to the pieces initially identified by <em>Digital Humanities Now</em>. Every article published includes the original date and location of publication, along with the date of revision, where applicable.</p>
<p>The <em>Journal of Digital Humanities </em>offers expanded coverage of the digital humanities in three ways. First, by publishing scholarly work beyond the traditional research article. Second, by selecting content from open and public discussions in the field. Third, by encouraging continued discussion through peer-to-peer review. Our May 2013 report on Reader Feedback is available <a href="http://pressforward.org/2013/05/reader-feedback-on-jdh/" target="_blank" title="Reader Feedback on JDH">here</a>.</p>
<p>The <em>Journal of Digital Humanities</em>, <em>Digital Humanities Now</em>, and the <a href="http://pressforward.org" target="_blank" title="PressForward Project Homepage">PressForward</a> project are produced by the<a href="http://chnm.gmu.edu/" target="_blank" title="Homepage for the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media at George Mason University"> Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media</a>.</p>

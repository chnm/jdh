<h2><a href="/3-2/going-meta-on-metadata/">Going Meta on Metadata</a></h2>
<p>I once joked to an archivist that all I really do as a historian is add <em>meta</em>-metadata to the archival database.</p>
<p>What I meant was that if we understand metadata—the information that accompanies artifacts—as not merely descriptive, but also already on its way to interpretation, then what is historical scholarship but a further extension of this elaboration of the evidentiary record? <a class="read-more" href="/3-2/going-meta-on-metadata/"><br/><br/>(Read more) </a></p>

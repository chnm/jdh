<h2><a href="/1-1/bookworm/">Review of Bookworm, produced by Benjamin Schmidt, Martin Camacho, et al.</a></h2>
<p><a href="http://bookworm.culturomics.org" target="_blank" title="Bookworm">Bookworm</a> is a tool that allows users to create visualizations charting the use of words or phrases in selected large corpora over specified periods of time. The software was developed by a group of researchers at the <a href="http://www.culturomics.org/cultural-observatory-at-harvard" target="_blank" title="Culturomics, the Cultural Observatory at Harvard">Harvard University Cultural Observatory</a>  as a follow-up to the 2010 project that resulted in <a href="http://www.sciencemag.org/content/early/2010/12/15/science.1199644" target="_blank" title='Michel Jean-Baptiste, et al. "Quantitative Analysis of Culture Using Millions of Digitized Books"'>a cover story in <em>Science</em></a>, the <a href="http://books.google.com/ngrams" target="_blank" title="GoogleBooks, Ngram Viewer">Google Books Ngram Viewer</a>, and the coining of the term <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culturomics" target="_blank" title='Wikipedia entry, "Culturomics"'>‘culturomics’</a>. <a class="read-more" href="/1-1/bookworm/"><br/><br/>(Read more) </a></p>

<h2><a href="/1-1/querypic/">Review of QueryPic, produced by Tim Sherratt</a></h2>
<p><a href="http://discontents.com.au/shed/hacks/querypic" target="_blank" title="QueryPic">QueryPic</a> is a graphical search summarizer that mines content in the <a href="http://trove.nla.gov.au/" target="_blank" title="Trove, the National Library of Australia Digitised Newspaper Collection">Trove</a> newspaper archive from the <a href="http://www.nla.gov.au/" target="_blank" title="National Library of Australia">National Library of Australia</a>. The program is part of Tim Sherratt’s larger <a href="http://wraggelabs.com/emporium/trove-tools/" target="_blank" title="Tools for Trove">TroveNewspaper software project</a>, which allows researchers to obtain parsable data from the Trove collection. <a class="read-more" href="/1-1/querypic/"><br/><br/>(Read more) </a></p>

<p>The academic study of games — from board games of strategy to online multi-player video games — challenges and disrupts epistemologies held dear in the humanities. Traditional scholarly products such as monographs and journal articles, and to a lesser degree blog posts, are meant to be passively read, whereas games are meant to be actively played. <a class="read-more" href="/1-2/games-and-historical-narratives-by-jeremy-antley/"><br/><br/>(Read more) </a></p>

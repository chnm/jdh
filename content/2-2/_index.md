<h3><a href="/2-2/expanding-communities-of-practice/">Expanding Communities of Practice</a></h3>
<h4 class="author-name">The Editors</h4>
<p>The materials featured in this sixth issue of the Journal of Digital Humanities expose “communities of practice” in digital humanities beyond the constellations of people and institutions directly engaged in experimental and digitally-inflected scholarship. Communities of practice, socially constructed groups that form around shared interests or crafts, often generate forms of tacit knowledge that circulate informally. […]</p>

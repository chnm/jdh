<p>The content of the <em>Journal of Digital Humanities </em>comes directly from the work that digital humanists around the world produce and share on the open web; there is no formal submission process.</p>
<p>The <em>Journal of Digital Humanities</em> selects, reviews, edits, and publishes the most valuable work identified by the weekly publication <a href="http://digitalhumanitiesnow.org/" target="_blank" title="Digital Humanities Now">Digital Humanities Now</a>. <em>Digital Humanities Now</em> highlights important scholarship and news items for digital humanists from the aggregated RSS feeds in our <a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheet/pub?hl=en_US&amp;hl=en_US&amp;key=0AucqXAIBhf_idGNlZzVjSGkxQU9XNU4yb0w1clMxeXc&amp;single=true&amp;gid=3&amp;output=html" target="_blank" title="Digital Humanities Compendium">Compendium of Digital Humanities</a>, automated searches, and information shared by the community at large on Twitter. Please consider joining the community of volunteer <a href="http://digitalhumanitiesnow.org/editors-corner/" target="_blank" title="Digital Humanities Now Editors-at-Large">Editors-at-Large</a> who help to surface and nominate the pieces that advance digital humanities scholarship and practice.</p>
<p>If you would like your work to be considered for the <em>Journal of Digital Humanities</em> and <em>Digital Humanities Now</em>, or your information has changed recently, please fill out the form below to join the <a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheet/pub?hl=en_US&amp;hl=en_US&amp;key=0AucqXAIBhf_idGNlZzVjSGkxQU9XNU4yb0w1clMxeXc&amp;single=true&amp;gid=4&amp;output=html" target="_blank" title="Digital Humanities Registry">Digital Humanities Registry</a> and be sure to include an RSS or Atom feed, or Twitter handle in the form below.</p>

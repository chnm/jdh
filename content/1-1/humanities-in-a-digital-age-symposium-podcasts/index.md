On November 11th, the University of Virginia’s [Institute of the Humanities and Global Cultures](http://www.virginia.edu/humanities/ "Website for the Institute of the Humanities and Global Cultures at the University of Virginia") hosted a daylong symposium on “The Humanities in a Digital Age.” The symposium included two panels—one on Access & Ownership and the other on Research & Teaching—and two keynote talks.

The first keynote was given by [Stephen Ramsay](http://english.unl.edu/faculty/profs/sramsay.html "Professional page for Stephen Ramsay at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln"), Associate Professor in the Department of English and Fellow in the Center for Digital Research in the Humanities at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln.

The second keynote was given by [Dan Cohen](http://www.dancohen.org/ "Home page for Dan Cohen's blog and professional website"), Associate Professor in the Department of History and Director of the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History in New Media at George Mason University.

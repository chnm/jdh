In this video, David Mimno discusses some of the different choices one can make in training models and what their implications are for efficiency, scalability, and topic quality, using the MALLET topic modeling package. This presentation was recorded on November 3, 2012 at the Maryland Institute for Technology as part of the [Topic Modeling Workshop](/2-1/the-details-by-david-mimno/mith.umd.edu/topicmodeling "MITH Workshop, 'Topic Modeling'"), sponsored by the National Endowment of the Humanities and MITH, at the University of Maryland. Slides are available [here](http://www.cs.princeton.edu/~mimno/slides/details.pdf "David Mimno, Slides for Topic Modeling Presentation").

Joan Fragaszy Troyano is a Research Assistant Professor at the Center for History and New Media at George Mason University. She currently is working on the development of the <a href="http://pressforward.org">PressForward</a> software platform to improve the aggregation, curation, and publication of scholarship, and is the managing editor of <em>Digital Humanities Now</em> and the <em>Journal of Digital Humanities</em>. Her own research is in visual culture and public understandings of the past. She holds a PhD in American Studies from George Washington University.

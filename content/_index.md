<h3><strong>The <i>Journal of Digital Humanities</i> is currently on hiatus.</strong></h3>
</div>
<p></p>
<div style="color: #222222;">An experiment in scholarly communication conducted by RRCHNM’s PressForward Project, <i>JDH</i> was born of the desire to publish the most interesting and innovative digital humanities gray literature. Funded by the first PressForward grant (2011-2014) from the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, JDH was closely linked to RRCHNM’s publication, <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Digital Humanities Now</span>. Metrics for <i>DHNow</i>’s blog posts, conference presentations and white papers were used to identify content for <i>JDH</i>, where it was then revised, expanded upon, and eventually published in quarterly issues.</div>
<p></p>
<div style="color: #222222;">As an in-house, experimental publication, <i>JDH</i> accomplished its mission to provide essential insight for launching similar publications at a wide range of research organizations, the core focus of PressForward’s second (2014-2015) and third (2015-2018) phases. With this role behind it, <i>JDH</i> is taking a break while we at RRCHNM plan a sustainable second act. Our hope is that <i>JDH</i> will re-emerge as a journal that continues to highlight work in digital humanities. Until then, we are at work on an analysis of <i>JDH</i>’s first two years, and will share that publicly on its completion. Previous issues will remain archived and available for download.</div>
